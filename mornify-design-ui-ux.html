Icon slots

Every icon: name is a placeholder. 22 unique slots:

logo · moon · sun · flame · flame-off · alarm · bell · freeze · check · plus · minus · arrow · chevron · back · share · invite · settings · home · group · chart · calendar · warning · offline · external

Nav icons 22px, inline 16px, hero 76–112px. Single stroke weight across the set.

Rules the design enforces

  • No sleep duration is ever displayed — only the two clock times.
  • No score, no quality rating, no health or medical language.
  • No line charts. A list, because we do not measure.
  • Streak counts consistency, never duration.
  • Group is additive — every screen works alone.
  • Streak state is local; only the group panel degrades offline.
  • Paywall fires on risk, never on a timer or at install.

09

Not designed, on purpose

  • Sleep stage graphs — we have no sensors. Drawing one would be a lie.
  • Global leaderboard — moderation and anti-cheat we can't own by 22 September.
  • Feed and comments — a reporting surface we have no capacity to staff.
  • Profile screens — anonymous auth, nothing to show.
  • Dark/light toggle — theme follows time of day, not preference. That's the concept.
  • Weekly recap — cut item #6 in the action plan. Add only if Gate 3 clears early.

If a screen isn't in this file, it isn't in v1. Adding one after 21 September breaks the feature freeze.
